## Service 1 — AI Agent Opportunity Assessment

### The Foundation of Smart AI Investment

**The challenge most businesses face:**  
You've heard that AI can automate workflows, save time, and reduce errors. But your business has dozens of workflows — and the AI tool landscape has hundreds of options. Where do you actually start? Which automation pays off? Which ones are traps?

The Opportunity Assessment answers those questions before you spend anything on implementation.

**What we do:**  
We run a structured discovery engagement — sessions with your team, analysis of your workflows, and a scoring methodology applied to every candidate opportunity — then deliver a comprehensive AI Agent Opportunity Assessment (AAOA) report.

**What you get:**

- Current-state workflow maps for 3–8 of your core processes
- Scored opportunity matrix across ROI potential, implementation effort, data readiness, and strategic fit
- Prioritized recommendations — what to build, in what order, and why
- Conceptual agent designs for the top 3 opportunities
- Rough effort and cost estimates to help you plan
- A sequenced implementation roadmap
- Readout presentation with Q&A

**Why it works:**  
Our discovery process is adapted from enterprise program management methodology — the same structured approach used to make six-figure technology investments in large organizations, calibrated for SMB operations. You get a defensible roadmap you own, regardless of who builds it.

## Service 2 — AI Agent Implementation

### From Blueprint to Production

**Built for:** Clients with a clear use case, either from an AAOA or their own prior analysis, ready to invest in building it.

**What we do:**  
End-to-end design, build, testing, and deployment of AI agents — integrated with your existing systems, tested by your team, and documented for long-term use.

**What's included in every implementation:**

- Detailed requirements and solution design workshop
- Agent architecture design — tool selection, data flows, integration map
- Build with 2 rounds of client review and feedback
- Integration with your existing systems (CRM, email, databases, APIs)
- User acceptance testing support
- Deployment to production
- Documentation: agent runbook, maintenance guide, integration diagram
- 30-day post-launch hypercare (bug fixes, minor adjustments)
- Handoff session and team training (up to 2 hours)

**How we build:**  
We work primarily with Claude API (Anthropic), n8n, Make, and Zapier for most workflow automations. For more complex use cases, we use custom Python or Node.js. We recommend the stack that best fits the use case — not the one we're most comfortable with.

**Pricing:**

| Complexity | What it covers | Range |
|------------|----------------|-------|
| Light | Single-function agent, 1–2 integrations | $8K–$18K |
| Standard | Multi-step workflow, 3–5 integrations | $18K–$40K |
| Complex | Multi-agent orchestration, 5+ integrations, custom logic | $40K–$80K+ |

*All implementations are scoped and quoted after a complimentary 60-min requirements session.*  
*Milestone-based billing: typically kickoff, UAT, and launch.*
