How We Work

Principles That Govern Every Engagement

Fixed-fee by default

You know what you're paying before work begins. No hourly ambiguity, no invoices that surprise you.

You own everything we build

Code, documentation, diagrams — all of it transfers to you at close. You are never dependent on us to keep your agents running.

Vendor-agnostic, always

We do not accept referral fees or commissions from any tool vendor. Recommendations are based on fit — full stop.

Honest scoping

If a project is outside our current capabilities or too risky to promise well, we'll say so and refer you to a better-suited resource rather than overpromise.

Progress is always visible

Every implementation engagement includes a shared project board — milestones, status, open decisions — updated weekly. No black-box consulting.

No scope creep by default

Scope beyond the signed SOW requires a change order before work begins. You will never be surprised by additional charges.
