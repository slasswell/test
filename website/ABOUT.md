## Founder Story Section

**Headline:** Why I Started This Business

For most of my career, I worked in enterprise technology — managing complex programs, designing processes across large organizations, and helping teams adapt to change. I watched large companies pour millions into digital transformation and AI initiatives, with entire teams dedicated to strategy, governance, and implementation.

Then I started talking to small business owners.

The contrast was striking. Operators running 30, 50, 100-person companies — managing real complexity, serving real customers — with none of the internal support that large organizations take for granted. They were hearing the same AI promises, but navigating the landscape alone, with no roadmap, no trusted guide, and tools designed for someone else.

The gap wasn't technology. It was access to structured thinking about *where* AI fits in a specific business, and people who could *build* it without charging enterprise rates.

That's the gap I'm here to close.

I bring the same rigor I applied in enterprise delivery — structured methodology, clear communication, stakeholder alignment, project governance — and apply it at a scale and price point that works for the businesses that need it most.

## What I Believe Section

**Headline:** The Principles Behind How We Work

**Principle 1 — Strategy before tools.**  
The right question isn't "which AI tool should we use?" It's "which workflows, if automated, would create the most value for this specific business?" We answer that question first.

**Principle 2 — SMBs deserve enterprise-quality delivery.**  
Small and mid-sized businesses shouldn't have to choose between rigorous methodology and accessible pricing. We've built a delivery model that provides both.

**Principle 3 — You should own what we build.**  
Every engagement ends with documentation, source code, and a trained team. You should never be dependent on us to keep your agents running.

**Principle 4 — Honest scoping is non-negotiable.**  
We'd rather tell you a project is too complex for our current team than overpromise and underdeliver. Referrals to better-fit partners happen when they should.

**Principle 5 — AI agents work best when humans design them.**  
The best AI implementations we've seen start with a human who deeply understands the workflow. Our discovery process is built around that insight.

## What We're Not Section

**Headline:** A Few Things We're Not (and Why It Matters)

**We're not a software vendor.** We don't have a product to sell you. Our recommendations are based on what fits your workflow — not what creates a license for us.

**We're not a generalist IT shop.** We focus specifically on AI agent strategy and implementation. If you need general IT support, we'll refer you to someone better suited.

**We're not building dependency.** Some agencies want to be the only ones who understand what they've built. We build for handoff from day one.

**We're not right for everyone.** If you're a 2,000-person company with a dedicated IT department looking for enterprise-scale AI infrastructure, there are better-suited firms. We're built for the businesses they overlook.
