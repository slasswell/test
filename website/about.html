The Story

Why I Started This Business

For most of my career, I worked at the intersection of technology and operations — managing complex programs, designing processes across large organizations, and helping teams absorb significant change without losing momentum. I worked on enterprise-scale projects with dedicated IT teams, governance boards, and budgets that could absorb mistakes.

"Then I started talking to small business owners. The contrast was striking."

Operators running 20-, 50-, 100-person businesses — carrying real complexity, serving real customers — with none of the internal support structure that large organizations take for granted. They were hearing the same AI promises. But they were navigating the landscape alone, with no roadmap, no guide, and tools designed for someone else's budget and someone else's IT team.

The gap wasn't technology. AI capabilities have crossed a threshold where genuinely useful automation is accessible to any business willing to invest thoughtfully. The gap was access to someone who would sit down, understand the actual workflow, build the ROI case in plain language, and then execute with discipline.

That's the gap I built this business to close.

The methodology I use here is the same I applied in enterprise delivery — structured intake, documented requirements, milestone-based execution, and a disciplined handoff. It's not glamorous. But it's the difference between a tool that sticks and a tool that sits in a drawer.

Background

Experience That Shows in the Work

My background isn't in AI research or software development. It's in making complex things work in the real world — on time, within scope, with stakeholders who have competing priorities and limited patience for ambiguity.

That background turns out to be exactly what AI agent implementation requires. The hardest parts of any agent deployment aren't technical. They're: identifying the right workflow to target, defining the boundary of what the agent should and shouldn't do, integrating it with systems already in use, and getting the team to trust and use it consistently. None of those problems are solved by a software subscription.

Direct experience with Microsoft Copilot Studio, Tines, and API-based integrations means I can translate between the technical and the operational — without requiring a developer in the room to move the conversation forward.

What I Believe

The Principles Behind Every Engagement

These aren't marketing copy. They're the things I actually live by in the work — shaped by military service, years of client delivery, and a conviction that the best long-term business relationships are built on honesty, not just outcomes.

1
Strategy before tools.

The right question isn't "which AI tool should we use?" It's "which of your workflows, if automated, would create the most real value?" We answer that first.

2
Tell the truth, even when it's hard.

If a project is too complex, too risky, or not the right fit for your business right now — you'll hear that from me directly, not in a passive non-answer.

3
You should own what we build together.

Every deliverable — the report, the blueprint, the code — belongs to you. I'm not interested in building dependency. I'm interested in building trust.

4
Human-first, always.

AI agents work alongside people, not instead of them. Every implementation I design keeps the human operator at the center — capable, informed, and in control.

5
Do right by the client.

This is a faith-informed conviction, not just a business principle. If the work I do doesn't genuinely improve your situation, I haven't done my job.

Honest Positioning

A Few Things This Business Is Not

Knowing what doesn't fit here helps you make a better decision about whether I'm the right partner.

Not a software vendor.

No product to sell. No licensing fees. No platform I'm financially incentivized to recommend. Tool recommendations are based entirely on what fits your workflow.

Not a generalist IT shop.

The focus here is AI agent strategy and implementation. For general IT support, infrastructure management, or helpdesk needs, I'll refer you to someone better suited.

Not building for dependency.

Some agencies want to be the only ones who understand what they've built. I build for maintainability and handoff from day one — your team should be able to run this without me.

Not the right fit for every business.

If you're a 1,500-person company with a dedicated AI team looking for enterprise-scale infrastructure, there are better-suited firms. I'm built for the businesses they overlook.
