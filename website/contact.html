Before You Reach Out

A Few Quick Answers

Is the discovery call really free?
Yes, entirely. No credit card, no hidden commitment. The 30-minute call is designed to help both of us decide whether working together makes sense. If it does, I'll explain what the logical first step looks like. If it doesn't, I'll tell you that honestly and point you somewhere useful.
What should I prepare before the call?
Nothing required. It helps to think about 1–2 workflows that feel the most repetitive, time-consuming, or error-prone. But you don't need to have a use case prepared, a budget decided, or a tech stack documented. We'll figure that out together.
What if I'm not sure my business is big enough?
If you have at least 5 employees and at least a few repetitive workflows that eat time, you're in the right place. Some of the best AI agent implementations are at small businesses where even modest time savings have a big operational impact.
Do you work with businesses outside my area?
Yes. All discovery sessions, Blueprint work, and implementation reviews happen over video call. I work with clients across the country. In-person is available for clients in Denver, CO — mention it when we talk.
