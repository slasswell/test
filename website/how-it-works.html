A Clear Path from "Where Do We Start?" to a Running Agent

Every engagement follows the same disciplined arc. No guessing, no skipped steps, no deliverables that sit in a drawer. Here's exactly what working together looks like.

The Philosophy

Methodology First. Tools Second.

Most AI consulting starts with a tool and works backward. We start with your workflows and work forward. The difference: you end up with an agent that solves a real problem, not a solution in search of one.

Our process is adapted from enterprise program management methodology — structured intake, documented requirements, milestone-based delivery, and a disciplined handoff. It's the same rigor used for six-figure enterprise technology programs, applied at a scale that works for businesses with 5 to 200 people.

The Discovery Call

Before any engagement begins, we spend 30 minutes together on a call. You'll tell me about your business. I'll ask about your biggest operational friction points, your tools, and what you've already tried. There's no pitch deck and no pressure. The goal is mutual: figure out if working together makes sense, and if so, where to start.

If it's a fit, I'll recommend which engagement tier makes sense and why. If it's not a fit, I'll tell you that too — and point you toward something more appropriate.

AI Readiness Assessment

This is where we do the work that makes everything else smarter. Before a single line of code is written — before any tool is recommended — we map what's actually happening in your business.

We run 2–3 structured discovery sessions with the people who know your workflows best (not just leadership — the people who actually do the work). We build process maps together, annotate the pain points and bottlenecks, and score every candidate opportunity on four dimensions: ROI potential, implementation complexity, data readiness, and strategic fit.

You receive the AI Agent Opportunity Report: a 10–20 page document that tells you what to automate, in what order, and why — with rough effort and cost estimates for each recommendation.

Then we do a live readout together so you can ask every question before any decision is made.

Agent Blueprint

The Blueprint takes your top 1–2 priorities from the Assessment and turns them into a complete, buildable implementation plan.

We define exactly what the agent will do, what systems it will touch, how data will flow, which tools we recommend and why, what integrations need to be set up, what the build timeline looks like milestone by milestone, and what the quantified ROI case is — using your own numbers.

You receive the Agent Blueprint Document: a 15–30 page specification detailed enough for any qualified builder to execute from. We walk through it together so there are no surprises before build begins.

Agent Build & Deploy

This is where we build the thing. Every build engagement is fixed-fee and milestone-based — you know exactly what you're paying and what you'll get at each stage.

We don't deliver a big reveal at the end. By the end of week 2 of any build, you'll see a working draft of the agent — even if it's incomplete. We run two structured review rounds where you test against a defined scenario list. Then we deploy to production, train your team, and hand over complete documentation.

Every build includes 30 days of post-launch hypercare: we fix bugs and make minor adjustments at no additional charge while your team settles in.

Retainer & Ongoing Support

AI agents are not set-and-forget. Upstream systems change. Business processes evolve. A model that performed well in January may behave differently in September. Without someone watching, agents drift — sometimes silently.

Our optional monthly retainer keeps your agents healthy, responds quickly when something breaks, and proactively surfaces new opportunities as your business grows. Common Questions

What People Usually Ask Before Starting

Do I have to start with the Assessment, or can I come with a specific use case?
Either works. If you already have a specific, well-scoped use case, we can move directly to a Blueprint or even a scoping call for the Build. The Assessment is most valuable when you're not sure where to focus, or when you want to make sure you're prioritizing the right opportunities before investing in implementation.
How much involvement does my team need to have?
For the Assessment, we need 2–3 hours of time from the people who know your workflows best — usually 1–3 team members per session. For the Build, we need a designated point of contact who can respond to questions and participate in the two review rounds. We're designed to work with lean teams; we don't require a project coordinator on your side.
What if the agent doesn't perform the way we expected after launch?
Every build includes 30 days of post-launch hypercare: we fix bugs and make minor adjustments at no extra charge. KPIs are defined and agreed before build begins, so we have a shared standard for "working as expected." Beyond hypercare, our retainer plans cover ongoing support and optimization.
What kinds of AI agents do you build?
We focus on workflow automation agents: intake processing, document summarization and routing, follow-up drafting, report generation, data entry and validation, scheduling coordination, and multi-step approval workflows. We work primarily with the Claude API (Anthropic), n8n, Make, Zapier, Tines, and Microsoft Copilot Studio, with custom Python or Node.js for more complex needs.
Will my team be able to maintain the agent after you're done?
Yes — that's a design principle, not an afterthought. Every build includes a runbook (what it does, how to monitor it, how to update it), an integration diagram, and a maintenance guide. We build for maintainability, not dependency. The training session at handoff is specifically designed to make your team confident managing the agent day-to-day.
How do you handle scope creep?
Every engagement starts with a signed Statement of Work. Any scope beyond what's in the SOW requires a signed change order before any additional work begins. We won't just absorb scope — and we won't surprise you with an invoice for work we didn't agree on. If you discover something new mid-engagement that you want to add, we scope it, price it, and get your approval first.
