# Service Packages

**AI Agent Consulting & Implementation**

---

## Overview

Services are structured in three tiers that mirror the natural progression of an AI adoption engagement: understand, build, sustain. Clients can enter at any tier, but most benefit most from starting with a Discovery engagement.

---

## Tier 1 — Discovery: AI Agent Opportunity Assessment

**What it is:** A structured consulting engagement that maps a client's workflows, identifies high-value automation opportunities, and delivers a prioritized AI Agent Opportunity Assessment (AAOA) report.

**Who it's for:** SMB leaders who know AI is relevant to their business but don't know where to start, or who want an independent perspective before committing to implementation spend.

**What's included:**
- Pre-engagement intake and stakeholder alignment call (1 hr)
- 2–3 workflow discovery sessions (1.5 hrs each) with key operators
- Process mapping of 3–5 target workflows
- AI opportunity scoring across dimensions: ROI potential, implementation effort, risk, data readiness
- Prioritized AAOA report (10–20 pages) with:
  - Executive summary and top recommendations
  - Workflow maps with pain point annotations
  - Scored opportunity matrix
  - Recommended agent architectures (conceptual, not technical)
  - Suggested sequencing and phasing
  - Rough effort and cost estimates for top 3 opportunities
- Readout presentation and Q&A session (1 hr)

**Delivery timeline:** 3–4 weeks

**Pricing:**

| Scope | Price |
|-------|-------|
| Focused (1–2 departments, up to 3 workflows) | $3,500 |
| Standard (2–3 departments, up to 5 workflows) | $6,000 |
| Comprehensive (3–5 departments, up to 8 workflows) | $9,500 |

**Notes:**
- Fixed-fee, no surprises
- 50% due at kickoff, 50% due at report delivery
- Discovery fee is credited toward a subsequent implementation engagement within 90 days

## Tier 2 — Implementation: AI Agent Build & Deploy

**What it is:** End-to-end design, build, testing, and deployment of one or more AI agents identified in the AAOA (or a clearly scoped client request).

**Who it's for:** Clients who have a clear use case — either from an AAOA or their own prior analysis — and are ready to invest in building it.

**What's included:**
- Detailed requirements and solution design workshop
- Agent architecture design (tool selection, data flows, integration map)
- Build and iterative testing (2 rounds of client review/feedback)
- Integration with existing systems (CRM, email, databases, APIs)
- User acceptance testing support
- Deployment to production environment
- Documentation: agent runbook, maintenance guide, integration diagram
- 30-day post-launch hypercare (bug fixes, minor adjustments)
- Handoff session and team training (up to 2 hrs)

**Delivery timeline:** 4–12 weeks (varies by complexity)

**Pricing:**

| Complexity | Description | Price Range |
|------------|-------------|-------------|
| Light | Single-function agent, 1–2 integrations, limited customization | $8,000–$18,000 |
| Standard | Multi-step agent workflow, 3–5 integrations, moderate customization | $18,000–$40,000 |
| Complex | Multi-agent orchestration, 5+ integrations, custom logic, data pipelines | $40,000–$80,000+ |

**Notes:**
- Scoped and quoted per engagement after a requirements session (complimentary, 60 min)
- Milestone-based billing: typically 3 milestones (kickoff, UAT, launch)
- Additional scope requires a signed change order; no surprise charges
- T&M available for well-defined engagements with uncertain scope — quoted separately

## Engagement Principles

1. **Fixed-fee by default.** Clients know what they're paying before we start. No hourly ambiguity.
2. **No lock-in.** The AAOA is a deliverable you own. You can take it anywhere. We earn your continued business by delivering results.
3. **Vendor-agnostic.** We recommend tools that fit your workflow — not tools we have a financial incentive to sell.
4. **Honest scoping.** If a project is too large or complex for us to deliver with confidence, we'll say so and refer you to the right resource.
5. **Progress visibility.** Every implementation engagement includes a shared project board with milestones, status, and open decisions — no black-box consulting.
