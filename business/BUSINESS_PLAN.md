## Executive Summary

This business provides end-to-end AI agent consulting and implementation services to small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs). We help organizations move from AI curiosity to measurable operational improvement — identifying where AI agents create real value, then building and deploying them.

The business operates in two intertwined functions:
1. **Discovery Consulting** — Structured engagements that assess a client's operations, identify high-value workflow automation opportunities, and produce a prioritized AI Agent Opportunity Assessment.
2. **Delivery/Implementation** — Design, build, deploy, and support of AI agents identified in the assessment phase.

The founder brings enterprise-grade program management, process design, and client delivery experience — applied to a market (SMBs) chronically underserved by AI vendors who target only large enterprises.

---

## Market Opportunity

### Target Market
- Small and medium-sized businesses (10–500 employees)
- Industries with high-volume repetitive workflows: professional services, logistics, healthcare administration, financial services, real estate, agencies
- Businesses currently using disconnected SaaS tools (CRM, email, project management, accounting) with no automation layer

### Market Gap
Most AI tooling and consulting is built for enterprises with dedicated IT and AI teams. SMBs are left to navigate a fragmented vendor landscape without the internal expertise to evaluate, implement, or maintain AI solutions. They need a trusted advisor who speaks their operational language — not just a technology vendor.

### Market Size
- 33M+ SMBs in the US; ~6M with 10–500 employees
- Addressable segment (those with digital workflows and budget for optimization): ~500K businesses
- Average consulting spend per engagement: $5K–$50K
- Conservative serviceable market: $500M+ annually

## Services

### Phase 1: Discovery Consulting
Structured engagements that produce a clear, prioritized roadmap of AI agent opportunities.

**Deliverable:** AI Agent Opportunity Assessment (AAOA)

### Phase 2: Implementation
Design, build, and deploy the AI agents identified in the AAOA. Engagements are scoped based on complexity of the selected use cases.

**Deliverables:** Working AI agents, integration documentation, team training, handoff support

### Phase 3: Ongoing Support (Retainer)
Monthly retainer for monitoring, iteration, and continued discovery as the client's operations evolve.

See `../services/SERVICE_PACKAGES.md` for detailed tier definitions and pricing.

## Business Model

### Revenue Streams

| Stream | Type | Notes |
|--------|------|-------|
| Discovery Consulting | Fixed-fee project | $3K–$10K per engagement |
| Implementation | Fixed-fee or T&M project | $10K–$75K per engagement |
| Ongoing Support Retainer | Monthly recurring | $1K–$5K/month |

### Unit Economics (Year 1 Target)
- Discovery engagements: 12/year @ $6K avg = $72K
- Implementation projects: 8/year @ $25K avg = $200K
- Retainers: 6 clients @ $2K/month = $144K
- **Target Year 1 Revenue: ~$416K**

### Pricing Philosophy
- Transparent, fixed-fee discovery engagements (no scope creep anxiety for clients)
- Value-based implementation pricing anchored to the ROI quantified in the AAOA
- Retainer pricing tied to ongoing agent count and complexity

## Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1 — Establish (Months 1–3)
- Launch website with clear service positioning and case study placeholders
- Publish 3–5 thought leadership pieces on AI agent use cases for SMBs
- Target 2 pilot clients (preferred: existing network referrals) at discounted rate in exchange for testimonials and case study rights
- Build referral relationships with bookkeepers, accountants, and business coaches who serve SMBs

### Phase 2 — Prove (Months 4–6)
- Complete 2–3 discovery engagements; convert at least 1 to implementation
- Develop 1–2 reusable agent templates from delivery work (reduces future implementation cost)
- Begin building a small partner ecosystem (AI tool vendors, integration specialists)
- Launch email newsletter targeting SMB operators

### Phase 3 — Scale (Months 7–12)
- Hire 1 part-time implementation specialist or engage trusted subcontractors
- Systematize the discovery process (reduce delivery time per engagement)
- Pursue speaking opportunities at SMB-focused conferences and events
- Add a self-serve diagnostic tool on website to generate inbound leads

### Lead Generation Channels
1. **Warm referrals** — Primary channel in Year 1; prior enterprise network + SMB advisor referrals
2. **Content marketing** — LinkedIn posts, case studies, a newsletter on AI for SMB operators
3. **Strategic partnerships** — Referral agreements with bookkeepers, accountants, HR consultants, and business coaches
4. **Speaking/events** — Local business associations, industry conferences
5. **Inbound (website)** — SEO content, diagnostic tool, lead magnet (AI Readiness Checklist)

## Competitive Landscape

### Competition
| Type | Example | Weakness |
|------|---------|---------|
| Enterprise AI Consultancies | Deloitte, Accenture | Priced out of SMB range; culturally misaligned |
| Generalist IT Consultants | Local MSPs | Lack AI-specific depth and agent expertise |
| SaaS Vendors | Zapier, Make | Tool-centric, not workflow-strategy-first |
| Freelancers | Upwork agents | No structured methodology; inconsistent quality |

### Competitive Advantage
- **Methodology-first:** Proprietary discovery process (AAOA) gives clients a trusted map before any spend
- **SMB fluency:** Communication and pricing designed for operators, not CTOs
- **Founder credibility:** Enterprise PM background delivers enterprise-quality delivery at SMB scale
- **Vendor-agnostic:** We recommend the right tool for the job, not a preferred vendor's product
